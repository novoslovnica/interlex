# Hunspell spell-checking dictionaries — 2026-09-24 (direction 6 of docs/strategy-directions-2026-09-19.md)

> Вынесено из AGENTS.md при реструктуризации 2026-09-26.

`scripts/export-hunspell.ts` builds `exports/hunspell/` (gitignored): `isv_Latn.{aff,dic}`, `isv_Cyrl.{aff,dic}`, `README.txt`, `metadata.json`, `isv-hunspell.zip`, `isv-spellcheck.oxt` (LibreOffice extension). Served by `GET /api/downloads/<file>` (whitelist in `lib/export/hunspell/files.ts`) and described on `/downloads`. On production: `bash prod.sh run scripts/export-hunspell.ts --apply` (manifest entry, read-only, ~35 s).

- **Forms**: `forEachLexemeForms` (`lib/corpus/tokenizer/analyzer-factory.ts`) — the same per-lexeme engine walk that `buildGeneratedFormIndex` uses, extracted so the two can't drift — over public lexemes with a meaning, plus `inflection_anomalies`; multi-word forms skipped; everything lowercased (`properNoun` is not yet reviewed, and a lowercase Hunspell entry accepts a capitalized word).
- **Orthography** (`lib/export/hunspell/orthography.ts`, since moved to `lib/orthography/standard.ts`): the engine emits etymological spelling (ę ų ė ȯ å ŕ ľ ń ť), people write the official standard — measured on the corpus: jezyk 9 459 vs język 349, sut 22 139 vs sųt 1 771, prijatelj/konj/stolětja, medžuslovjansky 3 887 vs međuslovjansky 38, and Cyrillic with ј (језык, сут). `lib/isv.ts`'s `isvToCyr`/`standardToSimple` and `lib/transliteration.ts` produce something else (іезык, iezyk) and are **not** used here. Soft consonants depend on position: before a vowel lj/nj/tj/dj/rj, before a consonant plain (međuslovjańsky → medžuslovjansky), word-final lj/nj but t/d/r (pęť → pet). The two scripts are treated alike (maintainer's requirement): ě is accepted as standard / simplified in both — člověk / človek, чловєк / чловек — plus the etymological Cyrillic ѣ (чловѣк; corpus: є 4 872 tokens, ѣ 570); Latin also y→i and optional ć/đ, Cyrillic ы/и, дж/џ, љ/лј, њ/нј, ћ/ђ. Etymological spellings (sę, język, sųt) are deliberately **not** accepted (maintainer's choice) — they are about half of what the dictionary still rejects in a corpus sample.
- **Compression** (`lib/export/hunspell/compress.ts`): per lexeme, longest common prefix + suffix set; identical sets share one `SFX` class (`FLAG num`, no conditions, strip 0, `NEEDAFFIX` when the stem is not a word). `expandHunspell` reverses it; the export script refuses to write files unless the expansion equals the collected forms exactly.
- **Measured** (2026-09-24, local copy): 20 845 lexemes, ~802 k Latin / ~875 k Cyrillic word forms, ~124 k / ~140 k .dic entries, 1 457 / 1 648 classes, 1.4 MB zipped. Accepted 97.2% of a 3 500-token sample of recognized ("green") corpus tokens, rejected English function words (checked with `nspell`, a JS Hunspell).
- **Fixed on the way**: `canonicalFromStem` (`lib/grammar/common/stem.ts`) — ~600 adverbs store a stem without the final vowel (`takoze` / `takož`), so the engine never produced the diacritic spelling (takože, ješče, několiko). It now completes the stem with the value's tail when the folded forms agree; the plain value stays as a recognition variant, the corpus form index is unchanged (590 663 keys before and after).
- **Open**: license of the distributed dictionary (not decided — `/downloads` is not linked from the footer or sitemap until it is); LibreOffice may not offer "isv" in its language list — needs a manual check; Firefox needs a signed add-on (AMO).

## Addendum 2026-09-26 — canonical etymological spelling + CC BY-SA 4.0

Two maintainer decisions, implemented same day:

1. **Canonical spelling is the etymological one** (`sę`, `język`, `sųt`, `međuslovjansky`); the standard/simplified spelling is accepted as an alternative — the previous policy (standard only, etymological rejected) is inverted. Rationale: the grammar engine emits etymological forms anyway, and etymological spellings were ~half of what the dictionary still rejected in a corpus sample.
   - `lib/orthography/standard.ts`: new `etymologicalSpelling()` (raw engine form lowercased, stress stripped, only etymological-alphabet letters and hyphen; rejects leftover jers ъ/ь and ǫ — there was a migration to ų) and `latinLexemeSpellings()` (etymological form as-is + `latinSpellings()` conversions). Cyrillic side unchanged (already accepted ѣ/џ/лј/нј variants).
   - `lib/export/hunspell/collectForms.ts` passes every engine form through the sanity check; rejected forms are skipped with a warning (first 20 shown), never fatal. In the 2026-09-26 local build **1 173 forms** were rejected (311 distinct) — engine garbage: legacy ǫ/ъ, alternation slashes (`dǫje/dǫnъi`), `!`-prefixed forms, one lexeme contaminated with Cyrillic ҏ. All of these were silently dropped before too (the `isPlainLatin` filter), so no regression — Cyrillic stats are bit-identical to the previous build.
   - The export self-check (`expandHunspell` == collected forms) still guards the pipeline.
2. **License: CC BY-SA 4.0** for the distributed dictionaries and the project's data as a whole. Full legal text embedded as a constant in `scripts/export-hunspell.ts`; `LICENSE.txt` is generated into `exports/hunspell/` and included in both archives; `metadata.json` gained `license`; README header carries the license line + URL; root `LICENSE` file created; `package.json` has `"license": "CC-BY-SA-4.0"`. This unblocked the links: `/downloads` added to `STATIC_PATHS` in `app/sitemap.ts` and a "Downloads" link in `components/Footer.tsx` (i18n key `common.footer.downloads`, all three locales).
